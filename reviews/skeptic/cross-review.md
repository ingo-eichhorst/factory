## Disputes and missing conditions to resolve

### Observation and source boundaries — CF01–CF05, CF19

**OB02 needs a projection qualifier.** OBT257 allows work to continue while a shed router removes observation; BD36 expressly describes active business paths outside monitor visibility. Therefore OB02 cannot imply unavailable execution. Separately track host service, business reachability, observer freshness, human receipt and control access. OBT201 already correctly says an independent alert ends blindness without restarting the host.

**BD04 needs occurrence-level meaning.** BDT030/S305 uses it for believing “failed” instead of “unknown”; BDT055/S330 uses it for selecting a misleading liveness claim. These may cause later wrong work, but are not themselves proof of such work. Restrict its merger with OP005 to actual work-product error.

**The apparent recovery disagreement resolves by naming functions.** Re-read `restore.rs::reconcile` retains leases and blocks retained running/attempted queued tasks. `reconnect.rs::reconnect_after_herdr_or_machine_restart` instead presumes gone when positive reconnection fails and records a replacement in an existing workspace. It does not launch one. `identifies_same_session` returns true when either harness UUID is absent. Thus conservative lease retention is real at one boundary, not proof of a product-wide death-evidence invariant. Actual overlapping writers still require subsequent launch and surviving write capability.

**S072 and S260 are different.** The delivery function commits intent, releases the transaction and calls its writer without a status reread. A queued cancel can commit in that gap: OP035 remains a library-possible interleaving, subject to caller serialization. The shipped writer is a manual handoff, not a demonstrated external send. S260's falsely successful durable stop is counterfactual to the inspected commit-error propagation. Neither proves an executed fault.

The reviews consistently distinguish mutable library tables from the event-store/plugin/saga target. Targeted source reinspection supports that caution, not a repository-wide absence claim. Fifteen `source-supported` rows are narrow transitions or counterexamples, not fifteen reproduced incidents.

### Persistence and feedback — CF08, CF10–CF12, CF17

Use the same background inputs when comparing basins. First remove the initiating outage while holding declared base demand and policy fixed; vary backlog and observe whether trajectories return, drain, cycle or escape. Then separately remove new arrivals to test work reproduction. **Drainage at zero demand cannot refute two basins at fixed positive demand.** Conversely, continued external overload cannot establish autonomous regeneration. No reviewed analysis measured recurrence, attraction, basin size or feedback gain.

Finite scope chains bound delegation depth. Continued growth beyond a finite tree needs a replenishing producer, fresh roots, repeated schedules or another explicitly permitted mechanism. Finite trees can still exhaust resources. Literal nontermination of correct intermediate steps is a different stopping-condition problem.

Do not merge the human loops: OP032 requires bad approvals to cause more approval-generating failures; OB34 requires dismissal to preserve the false-alert criterion; BD18 requires outstanding incidents/reminders to consume repair capacity. Mute policy and acknowledged deferral need neither loop. Prior audit SK01's notification reset cannot supply evidence that unresolved incidents were cleared here.

### History, meaning and enforcement — CF06–CF07, CF13–CF18

- **No unsafe action is not verified useful completion.** OP001 can mean ongoing work; OB01 includes truthful cancellation/rejection; GV01/BD01 require accepted bounded correctness. Refusing a request does not necessarily terminate its task or release its session.
- **Irreversible history is not necessarily damage.** OP028 includes ordinary authorized delivery. Duplicate charges may be financially remediable, although the historical duplicate remains. Keep the claimed lost property explicit.
- **A path, bytes, interpretation and accepted result are distinct.** OP034 can retain useful orphan bytes without completion. OP012 can retain completion evidence without original bytes. BD30's unavailable interpreter differs from GV39's lost business meaning.
- **S320 lacks an indexed residency-only branch.** Its prose correctly allows a prohibited geographic transfer without new unauthorized learning, but BD06/BD25 alone encode disclosure or effective refusal. Carry that noncompliance branch separately pending applicable law; do not fabricate a confidentiality loss or existing guard.
- **Local retirement is not participant closure.** GV46 can describe a usable export and stopped Factory services while BD32 still describes outside queued obligations. Final liabilities and monitoring-contract coverage are further dimensions.
- **Guards remain conditional capabilities.** Generic freshness, safety, semantic approval, aggregate resource isolation and participant fencing are not established by the local attempt-budget code. Record source-local behavior, design promise, hazard, proposed intervention and missing prerequisite separately. No proposed exit or permission rule becomes a demonstrated surviving structure merely by appearing in `survives`.
